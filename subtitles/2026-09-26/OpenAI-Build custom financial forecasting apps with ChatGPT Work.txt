For finance teams, closing the books is
only the beginning. The real work is
giving leadership and the board a clear
story and forecasting what comes next.
I'm asking chat GPT work to turn our
latest close into a site that will
include scenarios we can test together.
Sites gives me the capability to create
sharable interactive experiences that I
could use to communicate complex ideas
throughout the organization. And here it
is, the latest close, the drivers behind
it, and the scenarios we could use to
plan what comes next. In the scenario
lab, I could adjust assumptions and see
how they change the forecast.
Drivers and closed details show what is
driving the forecast and who owns each
follow-up.
And I've got a fully built out board
presentation, which I could take to my
next board meeting.
Now, what if I want to run some other
scenarios? We have a corporate
initiative called our 1010 scenario.
This is where we increase partner
contribution by 10% and decrease
expenses by 10%. Now, we can see how the
strategy changes the forecast and
whether it gets us back to plan. Finance
does more than report the numbers. With
chatpt work, teams can communicate the
story, test scenarios, and shape what
happens next.
