On one hand, A I tech stocks keep soaring, doubling in value, on the other, thousands of stocks see steady declines, even dipping below their prices from the crash before the nine twenty fourths rally. The A shares herding in the first half has pushed market divergence to the extreme, with capital mindlessly piling into A I computing chip and Optical Module sectors, whose valuations keep hitting record highs. But beneath this revelry, one single question weighs on everyone’s mind, where will this extreme tech herding lead, will it be a continuation of a long bull run, or a repeat of history’s mistakes? In A shares over thirty years, we have seen five rounds of extreme crowded trades. As for the current A I crowded trade, Niuniu will not make subjective predictions. I have picked three of the most telling, high value past cases to fully review, using history to answer the question on everyone’s mind right now, Once extreme herding ebbs, what will happen to A shares? if we were to pick the most iconic, most frenzied crowded trade in A shares history ... Nearly all veteran traders would say, the two thousand and six to two thousand and seven Five Golden Flowers bull run. Unlike today’s institution led hype around trending sectors, that was the A shares market’s first true instance of nationwide herding. No complex valuation logic, no obscure industry tales just one truth all folks believed in, Golden Flowers always rise, never sell, not even if you die! In two thousand five, the share structure reform took hold, unleashing years of pent up market vitality. With China's rapid urbanization, industrialization, and booming real estate infrastructure, the macro economy saw a golden age of growth. Hence, market funds flocked en masse to five core sectors, banking, insurance, real estate, coal, and non ferrous metals dubbed by the market the Five Golden Flowers. Now, a twenty percent mutual funds share counts as crowded trade. but back then, public funds’ holdings in finance and cyclical sectors neared a staggering forty eight percent. Nearly half of the market’s core capital tightly herded into those Five Golden Flowers. Among them, the non ferrous metals sector was the strongest herding theme back then, boasting an epic run. Yunnan Copper soared from six yuan to ninety eight yuan, and Shandong Gold rocketed from fifteen yuan to two hundred and thirty nine yuan! China Shipbuilding surged one hundred and forty fold in two years, stocks up ten or twenty times were everywhere. The rally boomed to such extremes that a surreal scene emerged in the market, every retail investor had a Periodic Table of the Elements in hand! In brokerage halls, retired chemistry teachers morphed into top tier Stock Gods, explaining the price surge of copper, aluminum, lead, and rare earths. Learn chemistry first, then trade stocks an oddity in A shares then. That was not even the wildest. In June two thousand seven, a stock market anthem a parody of Death Love blew up online, echoing through securities halls, internet cafes, and streets nationwide. No other song could so aptly sum up the herding faith of that era, in most investors’ minds, China’s golden economic decade had just kicked off. Weighty blue chip stocks are the nation’s core assets they only ever rise, never fall, hold them long term, and you can reap the rewards of economic growth. Back then, the best move was no move, hold stocks steady, cling to them and do not sell this became the go to play for retail folk and fund managers alike. But objectively speaking, two thousand seven was a full blown bull market even as the Five Golden Flowers thrived, other sectors fared quite well, too. Thus, the  Five Golden Flowers were not yet an investment credo. The turning point came in the wee hours of May thirty, two thousand seven. The market, with zero warning, saw a major policy shift, the stamp tax on securities trading was hiked from one to three percent. which investors aptly dubbed  The cock crows at midnight. That day, the market opened disastrously, the index plummeting six point five percent, with nearly nine hundred stocks hitting limit down, and most small cap theme stocks continued their daily limit down streak. In just a few days, retail investors lost not only all their prior gains, but also more than half their principal then, a scene unfolded that cemented the herding faith for good. The Five Golden Flowers, heavyweight blue chips, were virtually untouched. In just three trading days, finance, non ferrous metals, and coal fully rebounded to new highs. This plunge utterly reshaped the market’s landscape. Funds decisively shunned messy small caps, themes swarmed together, and core blue chips shifted from a broad bull market rally to a stark ninety tenths split. Later, while the index hit new highs daily, eighty percent of small cap stocks suffered a steady, silent slump, only those who stuck firm with the Five Golden Flowers could win effortlessly. On the sixteenth of october, two thousand seven, the Shanghai Composite Index hit six thousand one hundred and twenty four points, an A share record untouched for eighteen years. At its peak, no panic only revelry! Retail investors screamed  Hold six thousand, hit ten thousand! , institutions touted gains, funds chased highs, piling into blue chips. All believed the great bull market was far from over, yet none knew that this peak of mass euphoria would mark the end of a generation’s bull run. If six thousand one hundred and twenty four marked market frenzy, then PetroChina’s I P O was the ultimate tombstone for that crowded trade. Hailed as Asia’s most profitable firm, C N O O C’s I P O drew high hopes from all—countless retail investors poured out their savings and even borrowed to subscribe fully. On the fifth of November, two thousand seven, PetroChina listed at forty eight point six yuan—but little did anyone know, that first day price would become its all time high. Thereafter, PetroChina embarked on a decade plus long slide, tumbling from forty eight yuan all the way down to under four yuan. Countless retail investors who bought in at peak prices got stuck high and dry and even now, show no signs of breaking even. All binges must end and that end comes far faster than anyone might think. Amidst ongoing domestic rate hikes, peaking growth momentum, and the overseas subprime mortgage crisis erupting, the market began its steep plunge. The once invincible Five Golden Flowers herding totally unraveled, and heavyweight blue chip stocks all plummeted. The Shanghai Composite Index plunged from six thousand one hundred and twenty four points, all the way down to thousand six hundred and sixty four points in two thousand eight—a max drop of over seventy percent. Non ferrous and coal sector leaders saw max drawdowns over eighty five percent. Once, a nation’s faith turned into a nation’s scar. So, if the two thousand seven cyclical stock herding snared the sixties and seventies generation our parents ... Then, the twenty fifteen round of leveraged fund internet herding snared those born in the eighties and nineties. To this day, that herding episode remains the most surreal, wild, and blush inducing one in A shares history. Its launch coincided with the dawn of the four G network rollout and the smartphone boom. During China's economic transition, traditional Five Golden Flowers sectors faltered, leading the nation to champion mass entrepreneurship and innovation. Amidst ultra loose liquidity, institutional funds forged a rock solid consensus, the internet is the future it can change everything. For you watching, does that slogan ring a bell? So, the ChiNext board comprising computing, media, and telecom became the market’s absolute eye of the herding storm. You could say that back then, whether you raised pigs or sold eggs, if you had any link to the internet, your stock price would just take off! The most iconic event was the renaming farce of listed firm Dolun Stock. May twenty fifteen. this firm, which dealt in real estate and had zero link to the internet, sought to hop onto the hottest trend back then, P two P finance. they even changed the firm’s name to P two P! Even more absurdly, after the Shanghai Stock Exchange issued an inquiry letter, the firm’s official reply admitted, in black and white, “We currently have no viable business model, nor a P two P business team.” no concrete operational plans, the shift was merely at the idea stage. That year, fintech revenue was nil, yet the market cared not a whit. After that announcement, the stock hit several limit up days even unrelated firms were hyped this much! And firms directly tied to the internet? Even more so! Back then, the core of that herding play, LeEco, was the market’s faith based king, buoyed by that grand ecosystem narrative. Starting from a single video platform, it branched out into phones, T V's, cars, sports, and finance grabbing up nearly all the trending sectors. Institutions locked their positions, retail investors blindly held, and everyone online envisioned LeEco's sweeping internet empire. Back then, no one in the market cared about that outdated P E ratio, folks coined a new term, the Price to Dream Ratio! All investors were, alongside Boss Jia, suffocating for that dream. Now, if that were all there was to the tale, it would be just a run of the mill herding event, but what made this one’s ending so calamitous was leverage the ultimate catalyst. In twenty fifteen, margin financing ads popped up everywhere one to five, even one to ten leverage was commonplace. What does one ten even mean? It means if the stock drops ten percent, you get margin called! For the highly volatile internet sector, a ten percent swing was just business as usual each day. But back then, no one in the market cared about risk, all they saw were chances to get rich quick. To avert greater systemic risk, in mid twenty fifteen, regulators cracked down hard on shadow margin financing. The deleveraging alarm bells tolled abruptly, the finale was utterly brutal. A shares saw unprecedented mass consecutive limit downs. Leveraged investors were wiped out overnight, deep in debt. LeEco, retail investors' darling, saw its cash flow collapse, Jia Yueting fled overseas, leaving just an empty promise to return next week and a mess. The market, in the most extreme way, taught a generation of investors a harsh lesson, that the Dream Rate must revert to the P E ratio. Now, if those first two bubbles feel quite distant to you all, then the two thousand and nineteen to two thousand and twenty one drinking and drugging bubble I bet many of you comrades took part in that one! This was A shares first ever, super long rally fully steered by foreign and public funds and the first time stock trading morphed into a mass social craze of buying funds for wealth management. After weathering the twenty eighteen bear market sparked by the trade war, A shares shed their old playbook of betting on small, poor, or restructuring firms from the past twenty years. As A shares joined M S C I and other global indexes, vast sums of northbound foreign capital streamed in, sparking a whole new investment ethos. Buy only sector leaders, only core assets, only certainty this logic of the weak get cleared out, the strong stay strong took root deeply. Plus, with ongoing loose market liquidity, public funds saw their biggest boom ever. Funds heavily concentrated in leading consumer and Pharmaceuticals firms, starting the Maotai Index herding era. Kweichow Moutai soared from a twenty eighteen low of two hundred and thirty yuan to two thousand three hundred yuan in twenty twenty one. Mindray soared from twenty seven yuan to four hundred and seventy yuan. Online, young fund investors' screenshots boasting gains were everywhere. Campuses, dorms, office buildings, short video platforms everyone chatted about baijiu and meds, all sure that fearing highs meant a life of woe. And since most investors scored their first pot of gold via mutual funds, fund managers started breaking big into the mainstream. Young folks fangirled over fund managers -- across the land, even  fund manager fan club  popped up! Even if you were not buying funds, you surely heard that slogan, Kun Kun flies bravely, iKun will always follow! New fund investor all thought they had found the key to lifelong financial freedom. Just buy a star fund manager’s fund, and you can rake in cash while lying back—no more slaving away at a job and taking grief! Eventually, hot funds allocation rates were in single digits, only a handful from one hundred applicants got in. Gen Z never scrambled for salt or homes, but they did chase after their idol’s mutual funds! Yet, no tree can grow to the heavens. In twenty twenty one, post Chinese New Year, the horn for tightened liquidity sounded abruptly, that castle in the air, built on extreme valuations and a nationwide frenzy, crumbled in an instant. Baijiu and pharmaceuticals tumbled, the myth of core assets shattered under valuation’s iron fist. The Maotai Index, once held as gospel, faced a ruthless sell off. Countless herding stocks got slashed in half within mere months, and went on to set new lows for the next five years. This was not just wealth shrinking it was a whole generation's dream of a shortcut shattering. As history’s hands tick to the present, that same script now sports a shinier name. Spurred by a grand narrative, extreme herding once more charges forth, in the name of reshaping the future. All cry out,  This time is different! but the A shares market’s thirty plus years of history has coldly proven that while the themes of herding shift, human greed and fear never do. What folks firmly believe is this time is different will give way to a stark realization, each time is SAME! Any castle in the air that defies common sense will, once liquidity ebbs, be shattered to bits. Okay!
