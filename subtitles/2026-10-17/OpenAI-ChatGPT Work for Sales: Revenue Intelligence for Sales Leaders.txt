learning report built by ChatGPT work.

report ChatGPT work created for both web

seller activity, customer conversations,

sure I understand the key points of the
report before I dig in further.

we're hearing across our strategic
deals, broken down by category. This is
followed by our competitors' presence in
deals based on what's coming up in
customer calls.
As I scroll down, the report starts to
surface opportunities for our team.
Things customers are calling out and
areas where we may want to shift our
operational resources.

the field is performing.
What are our best reps doing and how can

rest of the team?

recommendations and actions that we can
use to refine our product strategy,
strengthen seller enablement, and
improve how we operate.

are the most common objections and
competitive threats in our strategic
deals? How are our best performing reps

performers? Should any of these
objections change our priorities? I want
examples."
It works through the underlying data and
gives me a detailed, well-cited set of
answers along with recommendations for

Now, let's turn those insights into
action. I'll use ChatGPT work to draft a
