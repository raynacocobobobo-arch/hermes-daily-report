Level one prevents agents overstating

Level two prevents agents from getting

Level three prevents agents getting the

Second, reviewers with none of the

Each one plays a real reader and gets

Their findings get checked against the
source, too, before anything changes.
Third, once the work is revised, brand
new reviewers who've never seen it check
the final version.
Each level adds to the one before as the
stakes go up.
