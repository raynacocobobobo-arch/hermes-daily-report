标题：ChatGPT Work for Sales: Account Planning and Meeting Prep
链接：https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hjXi7b01s2c

One of the biggest challenges for CROs
is ensuring consistent execution across
the sales organization, especially as
new sales plays are introduced.
At Open AI, we've deployed a personal
sales assistant to help every seller act
like a top seller.
Here, the seller asked the sales
assistant to plan my day.
Because it can draw on Slack, Google
Calendar, Salesforce, and other business
contexts, it identifies the seller's
highest priority actions.
This is more than an agenda. The
assistant recognizes that a Catalyst
workshop is scheduled for tomorrow, a
new sales play designed to build
customer interest and generate pipeline.
The assistant has automatically created
a command center site containing the
opportunity context, stakeholder
mapping, and workshop objectives.
A Catalyst workshop is a new sales play
and requires significant effort to
conduct it.
Most sellers don't have a dedicated team
of designers and creatives available on
demand. That is where the sales
assistant extends the seller's capacity.
The assistant has generated the assets,
including a PowerPoint that provides the
structure and content needed to lead the
workshop with customer-specific mobile
mock-ups. And the same assets are
reflected in the updated command center
site. This includes a narrative video
that adds animation and a compelling
preview the seller can share with the
customer to build interest before the
workshop. Finally, the seller packages
the materials and posts them to the
customer Slack channel so the entire
account team is prepared for tomorrow's
meeting.
As sales leaders introduce new sales
plays, the personalized sales assistant
helps embed execution into the operating
rhythm of the business.
That gives every seller the guidance and
context to perform like a top seller.
