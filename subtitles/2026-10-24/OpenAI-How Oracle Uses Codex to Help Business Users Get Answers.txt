标题：How Oracle Uses Codex to Help Business Users Get Answers
链接：https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ju-JyxDgV7g

If you asked me a year ago that you can actually describe an outcome to Codex
and it would be accomplished that would be inconceivable.
In OAL Oracle Applications Lab we you can think of us as part of the operating system
for the company. The way we answer questions in the past is via the use of reports. So
it's a laborious process to create a report to answer a question. Now we allow business
users to just describe an outcome and then it's really up to Codex to plan
and to rationalize what MCP service to call. They would gather the information and then
be able to then present the result that is being requested. It could be in the form of a analysis,
a report or even an application. That is a major shift. I would even call it business
transformation that would allow our business users to be able to make decisions a lot quicker.
