We're making it easier to keep data
and Sites up to date in two ways.
First, we now have a native way
to link scheduled tasks to a site
so that you can update
the site's data automatically.
Previously, you had to create
scheduled tasks separately,
and it wasn't necessarily
easy to keep track of.
But now, you can connect
Sites to a scheduled task,
and the Sites plugin will also suggest
it proactively when it makes sense.
Second, bring your own plugin.
With this new feature, you can build
a site that works with each visitor's
own app connectors and data.
That means you can create new kinds
of apps that have a personalized
view for each visitor
out of the box without having
to set up any third-party integrations.
When visitors of the same org access
a site, data will be refreshed
using their connectors after
they've granted the right access.
There's many things you could
build with this, anything that
requires custom views for the apps
you already use in your workspace.
For example, you could build a team
mission control site that lists
the team's priorities and shows the PRs
that each team member needs to review
or the Linear tickets assigned to them.
With the new Sites updates,
we're making Sites more personal.
You can build one site for your team,
keep the data up-to-date thanks
to scheduled tasks, and give
everyone a view of their own work
with bring your own plugin.
If you want to try it out,
just ask Codex.
