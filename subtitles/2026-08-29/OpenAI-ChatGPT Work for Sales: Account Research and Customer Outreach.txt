标题：ChatGPT Work for Sales: Account Research and Customer Outreach
链接：https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5DbZAMGZ5oM

At Open AI, our focus for sales is
simple. Redesign revenue workflows so
agents can carry more of the load and
let our sellers focus on driving deals
forward. In this example, an always-on
ChatGPT work agent has detected a signal
in one of my accounts based on updates
from the company's latest earnings
report. It gives me a link to the
earnings call if I want to dig through
it line by line. Even more helpful,
ChatGPT work generates an account brief
that combines that information with
recommended actions for breaking into
the account. The brief pulls together
key information about the company,
identifying the points that matter to
our sellers, and highlighting exactly
what the customer said. Pulled directly
from the source, that can help drive the
sales motion forward. It also generates
a stakeholder map with profiles that
help us understand our buyers, identify
the buying committee, and determine who
we need to win over to win the deal.
Finally, it generates an outbound email
for the team to send to our strongest
stakeholder.
Let's open that in Gmail and take a
quick scan through it.
The next step happens when the prospect
books the meeting. Because ChatGPT work
is connected directly to my calendar, as
soon as the meeting is booked, it begins
creating the assets the seller will
need. Here, you can see a polished
presentation based on our first call
pitch deck template. It incorporates key
elements from the earning signal and
turns them into relevant action-oriented
content for the meeting.
After the meeting, a ChatGPT work agent
automatically looks for the call
transcript and generates a review of the
conversation.
It updates the seller's CRM records,
suggests content for the follow-up
email, and even recommends the next
actions the team should take. This is
how we've redesigned revenue workflows
at Open AI. ChatGPT work agents connect
every stage, from detecting the signal
to managing the follow-up and everywhere
in between. So, our sellers can spend
more time building relationships and
moving opportunities forward.
